# ZkIdentityStepIllustration Genesis

## Inspiration

Backfilled from `lib/design_system/src/zk_identity_step_illustration.dart`, tests, and Widgetbook coverage during DS harness standardization.

## Design Decisions

- Keeps ZK identity step art as a custom DS illustration because M3 has no equivalent.
- Animation remains internal and decorative.
- Uses tokenized sizing and semantic visual language so it can support multiple identity steps.
