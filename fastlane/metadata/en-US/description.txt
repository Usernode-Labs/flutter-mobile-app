Make and share small apps with friends and groups.

Homeroom is a place for communities to make the small apps they need together: a sign-up sheet for the club, a game for the group chat, a tracker for the team.

How it works
- Make an app for you, your friends or your group.
- Use and improve apps with others.
- Suggest a change, try it as a preview, and vote it in. Nothing changes until the group agrees.
- Discover apps other groups have made and join the ones you like.

Safe by design
- Every app can be reported, and you can block apps and people.
- Every app is held to the same content rules as Homeroom itself.
- Previews of a change are visible only to that app's members.
