# ZkIdentityStatusCard Genesis

## Inspiration

Backfilled from `lib/design_system/src/zk_identity_status_card.dart`, tests, and Widgetbook coverage during DS harness standardization.

## Design Decisions

- Summarizes ZK identity status as a reusable card with plain step data.
- Keeps verification state mapping outside the widget.
- Uses semantic status treatment and tokenized spacing for compact trust feedback.
