# ShimmerCardSkeleton Genesis

## Inspiration

Backfilled from `lib/design_system/src/shimmer_card_skeleton.dart`, tests, and Widgetbook coverage during DS harness standardization.

## Design Decisions

- Packages common card loading structure on top of shared shimmer primitives.
- Avoids feature-specific placeholder content while preserving card rhythm.
- Uses DS surface and spacing tokens to align with loaded card layouts.
